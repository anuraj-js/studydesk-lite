Welcome to StudyDesk Lite

StudyDesk Lite is your personal study dashboard. It works perfectly without an internet connection and keeps all your data safely on your device. Here is a simple guide to help you use every feature.

  • Greeting & Clock: See a motivational message based on the time of day and a live clock. You can click the button under the clock to switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time.
  • Your Stats: Quickly see how many tasks you have left, how many you've finished, and the days remaining until your next exam.
  • Calendar: See your tasks and exams laid out on a calendar. Click any highlighted day to see exactly what is due, and use the arrows to look at future months.
  • Adding Tasks: Click "Add Task" to enter what you need to do. You can set a priority level and a due date so you never forget it.
  • Managing Tasks: Check the box to mark a task as done. Use the Edit button to change details or the Delete button to remove it.
  • Finding Tasks: Use the tabs to filter by Active, Completed, or Overdue. You can also type in the search bar to instantly find a specific task.
  • Reordering: You can change the order of your tasks. Just click and hold the six-dot handle on the left side of a task, then drag it up or down.
  • Adding Exams: Create an exam plan by entering the subject, name, date, and time.
  • Live Countdown: Every exam card shows exactly how many days, hours, and minutes are left until the exam starts.
  • Topic Tracking: Break your studying into smaller topics. Add a topic, then check it off when you've studied it. The progress bar will fill up automatically.
  • Managing Topics: Just like tasks, you can use the pencil icon to edit a topic's name, the X icon to delete it, or the six-dot handle to drag and reorder them.
  • Focus Sessions: Use the timer to focus on studying. Click Start to begin, and the app will automatically count your completed sessions.
  • Customizing Time: Open the settings menu to change how long you want to focus and how long your breaks should be. Click "Save Settings" to apply your changes.
  • Alerts: When the timer finishes, the app will play a sound and flash the screen to let you know it's time for a break (or time to get back to work).
  • Writing Notes: Create a new note, give it a title, and start typing. The app saves your notes automatically as you type, so you never lose your work.
  • Finding Notes: Use the search bar in the sidebar to find notes by their title or the text inside them.
  • Quick Save: If you want to save manually, just press Ctrl + S (or Cmd + S on Mac).
  • Deleting: When you delete a note, a popup will appear first to make sure you don't delete it by accident.
  • Display Name: Change the name used in your greeting. Press Enter on your keyboard to save it, or Escape to cancel.
  • Data Backup: You can save a copy of all your data to a file on your computer (Export), or load a previous copy (Import). This is useful if you want to move your data to a new device.
  • Danger Zone: If you want a fresh start, this button will permanently erase all your tasks, exams, and notes.
  • No Internet Needed: You can use StudyDesk Lite anywhere, even on a plane or without Wi-Fi, because everything is saved directly on your device.
  • Install on Phone: If you are using a phone, you can add StudyDesk to your home screen. Tap your browser's menu and select "Add to Home Screen" so it feels like a real app.
  • Keep Your Data Safe: All your data is saved inside your web browser. If you clear your browser history or cache, you might lose your tasks and notes. To be safe, use the Export button in Settings to save a backup file occasionally.
