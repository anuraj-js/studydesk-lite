Welcome to StudyDesk Lite

StudyDesk Lite is your lightweight, offline-first academic dashboard. Here is a quick guide to help you get the most out of the app.

  • Dynamic Greeting: A motivational message that changes based on the time of day.
  • Live Clock: Toggle between 12-hour and 24-hour formats.
  • Stats Grid: See your pending tasks, completed tasks, next exam countdown, and today's focus sessions.
  • Calendar Widget: Click on any highlighted day to see the tasks and exams due on that date.
  • Adding Tasks: Enter a title, choose a priority, and optionally set a future due date.
  • Filtering: Filter tasks by All, Active, Completed, or Overdue.
  • Search: Use the search bar to find tasks by title instantly.
  • Quick Actions: Check the box to mark a task complete, or use the Edit/Delete buttons to manage your list.
  • Live Countdown: Each exam card shows a live, second-by-second countdown.
  • Topic Tracking: Break down your exam preparation by adding specific topics and checking them off as you study.
  • Search: Filter your exams by subject or name.
  • Cascading Delete: Deleting an exam also permanently deletes all its associated topics.
  • Timer: Start, pause, or reset the focus timer. The browser tab title will update with the remaining time.
  • Custom Settings: Change the duration of Focus, Short Break, and Long Break sessions. Click Save Settings to apply them.
  • Session Counter: The app automatically counts your completed focus sessions for the day.
  • Auto-Save: Notes save automatically 600ms after you stop typing.
  • Search: Find notes by title or body content in the sidebar.
  • Safe Deletion: Delete notes via a custom confirmation dialog to prevent accidental data loss.
  • Display Name: Change the name shown in the greeting and sidebar.
  • Data Backup: Export all your data to a JSON file to move it to another device, or Import a previous backup.
  • Danger Zone: Permanently wipe all data from the app.
